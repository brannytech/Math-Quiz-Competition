Quiz Rules

  • Each question has a maximum response time of 60 seconds.
  • The Quiz Master controls the computer and confirms the participant's selected answer.
  • Once a question has been selected, it becomes unavailable to other participants.
  • A correct answer receives a positive response; an incorrect answer receives a wrong-answer alert.
  • If a participant answers wrongly, or does not answer within 60 seconds, the question passes to the opposing participant.
  • The opposing participant then has 30 seconds to answer.
  • The correct answer is only revealed when the Quiz Master clicks "Show Answer" — this lets the audience keep working the problem out.
  • If the main round ends in a tie, use the separate Tie-Breaker board of 10 questions.
  • Participants must wait for the Quiz Master to confirm and select their chosen option.
  • The Quiz Master's decision on answer selection and timing is final.
